Initial evaluation of positive hepatitis serology in adults, HBsAg-positive*
This algorithm is intended for use with additional UpToDate content on acute and chronic hepatitis and HBV vaccination.

anti-HBc: hepatitis B core antibody; anti-HBe: hepatitis B e antibody; anti-HBs: hepatitis B surface antibody; CBC: complete blood count; HAV: hepatitis A virus; HBeAg: hepatitis B e antigen; HBsAg: hepatitis B surface antigen; HBV: hepatitis B virus; HCV: hepatitis C virus; HDV: hepatitis D (delta) virus; IgG: immunoglobulin G; IgM: immunoglobulin M; INR: international normalized ratio.

* Hepatitis B serologies (HBsAg, anti-HBs, total anti-HBc) are reported as either positive or negative. Interpretation of a specific abnormal test result should be based on the reference range reported with that result.

¶ Appropriate measures to prevent transmission of HBV include:
  • Patient education
  • Hepatitis B vaccination to all nonimmune household and sexual contacts
  • Treatment and follow-up of personnel after occupational exposure to blood and body fluids

Δ Serologic tests indicative of chronic HBV infection:

  • Elevated or fluctuating alanine aminotransferase levels (usually 2 times the upper limit of normal but during an exacerbation can be >10 times the upper limit of normal)
  • Persistent HBV DNA
  • Variable HBeAg persistence
  • Persistent HBsAg
  • Undetectable anti-HBs

◊ Inactive chronic hepatitis is characterized by:

  • Absence of clinical manifestations of hepatitis
  • Positive HBsAg >6 months
  • HBeAg negative, anti-HBe positive
  • Normal transaminases
  • Undetectable or very low DNA

These patients still require monitoring of HBV DNA, which may be undetectable or very low, and alanine aminotransferase, which should remain normal.

§ Recovery is characterized by:
  • Resolution of clinical manifestations of acute hepatitis
  • Normalization of serum alanine aminotransferase
  • Negative or low titer HBV DNA
  • Seroconversion of HBeAg to anti-HBe
  • Switch from IgM to IgG anti-HBc
  • Subsequent seroconversion of HBsAg to anti-HBs
