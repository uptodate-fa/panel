Initial evaluation of high potassium in adults*
This algorithm is intended for use with additional UpToDate content on hyperkalemia.
K: potassium; ECG: electrocardiogram; ESKD: end-stage kidney disease; WBC: white blood cell count.
* The normal reference range for potassium is approximately 3.5 to 5.0 mEq/L. Interpretation of a specific abnormal test result should be based upon the reference range reported with that result.
¶ Life-saving interventions should not be delayed while awaiting the results of diagnostic testing.
Δ ECG changes of hyperkalemia include tall peaked T waves, PR prolongation, loss of P waves, QRS widening, ventricular fibrillation, and asystole.
◊ Medications associated with hyperkalemia include:
  • Angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor (ACE-I)/angiotensin-receptor blockers (ARB), amiloride, nonselective betablockers
  • Spironolactone, digitalis overdose
  • Trimethoprim/sulfamethoxazole
  • Cyclosporine, tacrolimus
  • Nonsteroidal antiinflammatory drugs (NSAIDS)
  • Octreotide
