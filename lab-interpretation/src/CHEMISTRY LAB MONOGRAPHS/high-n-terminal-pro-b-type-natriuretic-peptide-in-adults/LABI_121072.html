NT-proBNP thresholds based on eGFR and age*
  High NT-proBNP¶ Intermediate NT-proBNPΔ Low NT-proBNP◊
eGFR >60 mL/min/1.73 m2
  • 18 to 49 years of age: ≥450 pg/mL
  • 50 or more years of age: ≥900 pg/mL
  • 18 to 49 years of age: 301 to 449 pg/mL
  • 50 or more years of age: 301 to 899 pg/mL
  • 18 years of age or older: ≤300 pg/mL
eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m2
  • 18 years of age or older: ≥1200 pg/mL
  • 18 years of age or older: <1200 pg/mL
  • Insufficient data
NT-proBNP: N-terminal pro-brain natriuretic peptide; eGFR: estimated glomerular filtration rate; HF: heart failure.
* NT-proBNP levels are most useful in patients with an intermediate pretest probability of HF (eg, symptoms of dyspnea in a patient with predisposing conditions for HF, no evidence of fluid retention).
¶ High NT-proBNP levels support a diagnosis of acute HF.
Δ Intermediate NT-proBNP levels are neither sensitive nor specific for detecting or excluding acute HF. Other diagnoses, such as pulmonary embolism, left ventricular dysfunction without exacerbation, and cor pulmonale, should also be considered in patients with NT-proBNP in this range.
◊ In general, NT-proBNP levels <300 pg/mL are optimal for excluding a diagnosis of HF.
