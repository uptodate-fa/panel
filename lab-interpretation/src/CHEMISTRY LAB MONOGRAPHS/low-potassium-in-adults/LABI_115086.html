Initial evaluation of low potassium in adults*
This algorithm is intended to use in conjunction with additional UpToDate content on low potassium.
BUN: blood urea nitrogen; ECG: electrocardiogram; GI: gastrointestinal; PAC: plasma aldosterone concentration; PRA: plasma renin activity.
* The normal range for serum potassium is approximately 3.5 to 5 mEq/L (mmol/L). Interpretation of a specific abnormal test result should be based upon the reference range reported with that result.
¶ Symptomatic potassium depletion is often associated with multiple biochemical abnormalities (eg, hypomagnesemia, hypocalcemia, and metabolic alkalosis), which may contribute to clinical findings. Patients with concurrent hypomagnesemia can be refractory to potassium replacement alone.
Δ Refer to additional UpToDate table on the causes of hypokalemia.
◊ Serum potassium should be corrected to >3.9 mEq/L prior to measuring PAC:PRA ratio.
