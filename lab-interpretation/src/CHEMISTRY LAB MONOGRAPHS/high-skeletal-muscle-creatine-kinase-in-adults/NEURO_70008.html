Major causes of myopathy
Inflammatory Drugs and toxins
  • Polymyositis
  • Dermatomyositis
  • Inclusion body myositis
  • Juvenile dermatomyositis
  • Sarcoidosis
  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Sjögren's disease
  • Antisynthetase syndrome
  • Immune-mediated necrotizing myopathy
  • Vasculitis
  • Overlap syndromes – Systemic lupus erythematosus, systemic sclerosis, mixed connective tissue disease, rheumatoid arthritis, Sjögren's disease
  • Illicit drugs – Cocaine, heroin
  • Alcohol
  • Glucocorticoids
  • Other – Colchicine, antimalarial drugs, HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors, penicillamine, zidovudine
  •  
Infections
  • Viral – Influenza, parainfluenza, Coxsackie, HIV, cytomegalovirus, echovirus, adenovirus, Epstein-Barr virus
  • Bacterial – Pyomyositis, Lyme myositis
  • Fungal
  • Parasitic – Trichinosis, toxoplasmosis
  •  
Endocrine disorders Rhabdomyolysis
  • Hypothyroidism
  • Hyperthyroidism
  • Cushing syndrome (or exogenous steroid administration)
  • Crush trauma
  • Metabolic myopathies
  • Seizures
  • Alcohol abuse, including hyperkinetic state with delirium tremens
  • Exertion, especially with environmental heat illness
  • Vascular surgery
  • Malignant hyperthermia
Electrolyte disorders
  • Hypokalemia
  • Hypophosphatemia
  • Hypocalcemia
  • Hypernatremia or hyponatremia
Metabolic myopathies Inherited myopathies
  • Disorders of carbohydrate and lipid metabolism
  • Acid maltase deficiency
  • Muscular dystrophy
HIV: human immunodeficiency virus; HMG-CoA: beta-hydroxy beta-methylglutaryl-coenzyme A.
