Causes of acquired antithrombin deficiency*
  • Heparin
  • Hormones (estrogen-progestin oral contraceptives)
  • Cirrhosis
  • Acute thrombosis
  • Disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC)
  • Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO)
  • L-asparaginase
  • Major surgery
  • Pregnancy-induced hypertension, preeclampsia, eclampsia
  • Nephrotic syndrome
* A diagnosis of hereditary antithrombin deficiency cannot be made in the presence of one of these disorders.
