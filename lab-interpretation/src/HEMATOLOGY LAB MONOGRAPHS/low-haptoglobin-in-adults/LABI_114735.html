Initial evaluation of low haptoglobin in adults*
This algorithm is intended to be used with additional UpToDate content on hemolytic anemia.
CBCs: complete blood counts; AHTR: acute hemolytic transfusion reaction; TTP: thrombotic thrombocytopenia purpura; DIC: disseminated intravascular coagulation; HELLP: Hemolysis, Elevated Liver enzymes, Low Platelet count; APS: antiphospholipid syndrome; PT: prothrombin time; aPTT: activated thromboplastin time; LDH: lactic dehydrogenase; BUN: blood urea nitrogen.
* Normal reference range for serum haptoglobin is approximately 83 to 267 mg/dL (8.3 to 26.7 micromol/L). Interpretation of a specific abnormal result should be based upon the reference range reported by the laboratory.
¶ Clinical history, laboratory results, and peripheral blood smear findings guide further testing and urgency of specialty consultation.
Δ Review of peripheral blood smear by an experienced individual may identify abnormalities not detected by automated machines.
◊ Life-saving interventions should not be delayed while awaiting the results of diagnostic testing.
§ Liver biochemical tests include total and indirect bilirubin, alanine aminotransferase (ALT), and aspartate aminotransferase (AST).
¥ Refer to UpToDate table on causes of intra- and extravascular hemolysis.
