Supporting data come from published reports, identification of drug-dependent platelet-reactive antibodies, or both.[1] Certain foods and beverages (certain herbal teas, walnuts) can also cause thrombocytopenia. For most drugs, the mechanism is drug-induced immune thrombocytopenia (DITP) in which the antibodies are drug-dependent and require the drug to be present. Drug-dependent platelet-reactive antibodies have been detected by one or more laboratories for almost all of the drugs listed. The most commonly implicated drugs are shown in bold.[2-4] DITP may occur with drugs not listed herein. Additional drugs may cause thrombocytopenia along with other cytopenias by other mechanisms such as bone marrow suppression.
