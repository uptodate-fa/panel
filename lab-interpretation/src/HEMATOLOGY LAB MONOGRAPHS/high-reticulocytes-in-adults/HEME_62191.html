Causes of hemolysis that may be present in a patient receiving a transfusion
Immune-mediated
  • Acute hemolytic transfusion reaction (AHTR)
  • Delayed hemolytic transfusion reaction (DHTR)
Non-immune-mediated
  • Thermal injury
  • Heat
  • Cold
  • Osmotic injury
  • Mechanical injury
Infection
Congenital hemolytic anemia
Acquired hemolytic anemia
Drug-induced hemolytic anemia
Refer to UpToDate for discussion of hemolytic transfusion reactions. Some of these conditions may occur in a patient receiving a transfusion but are not caused by the transfusion.
