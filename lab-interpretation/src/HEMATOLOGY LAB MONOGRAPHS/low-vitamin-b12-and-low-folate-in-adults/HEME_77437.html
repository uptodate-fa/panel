Causes of vitamin B12 deficiency
Gastric abnormalities (site of intrinsic factor production)
Autoantibodies to intrinsic factor or gastric parietal cells (ie, pernicious anemia)
Gastrectomy/bariatric surgery
Gastritis
Autoimmune metaplastic atrophic gastritis
Small bowel disease (site of vitamin B12 absorption)
Malabsorption syndrome
Ileal resection or bypass
Inflammatory bowel disease (eg, Crohn disease)
Celiac disease
Bacterial overgrowth
Blind loop
Dibothriocephalus latus (fish tapeworm)
Pancreatitis
Pancreatic insufficiency
Dietary
Breastfed infant of a mother with vitamin B12 deficiency
Vegan diet
Strict vegetarian diet
Limited intake of animal protein during pregnancy or lactation
Agents that block or impair absorption or metabolism
Neomycin
Biguanides (eg, metformin)
Proton pump inhibitors (eg, omeprazole)
Histamine 2 receptor antagonists (eg, cimetidine)
Nitrous oxide (N2O) gas, used for anesthesia or recreationally
Genetic/hereditary
Transcobalamin II (TCII) deficiency due to biallelic germline pathogenic variants in TCN2
Imerslund-Gräsbeck syndrome due to germline biallelic pathogenic variants in CUBN or AMN, which encode components of the ileal receptor for the vitamin B12-intrinsic factor complex
Juvenile cobalamin deficiency due to germline biallelic pathogenic variants in CBLIF, which encode intrinsic factor
Pathogenic variants in genes involved in intracellular vitamin B12 metabolism (eg, LMBRD1, MMACHC); note that serum vitamin B12 levels may be normal
Refer to UpToDate topics on vitamin B12 and folate deficiency for further information on evaluation and management.
