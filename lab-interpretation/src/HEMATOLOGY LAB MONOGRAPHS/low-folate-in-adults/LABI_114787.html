Initial evaluation of low folate in adults*
This algorithm is intended for use in conjunction with additional UpToDate content on vitamin B12 and folate deficiency.
CBC: complete blood count; MMA: methylmalonic acid.
* Normal folate reference range is approximately >4 ng/mL (>9.1 nmol/L). Interpretation of a specific abnormal test result should be based upon the reference range reported with that result.
¶ Refer to UpToDate lab interpretation monograph and algorithm on low vitamin B12.
Δ MMA and homocysteine are intermediates in folate and homocysteine metabolism.
