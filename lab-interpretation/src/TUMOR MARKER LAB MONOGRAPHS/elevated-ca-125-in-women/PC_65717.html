Risk assessment criteria for inherited breast-ovarian cancer syndome, combining several guidelines
Non-Jewish families
Any of the following:
1 case of breast cancer ≤40 years old in a first- or second-degree relative
1 first- or second-degree relative with breast and ovarian cancer, at any age
2 or more cases of breast cancer in first- or second-degree relatives if one is diagnosed at ≤50 years old or is bilateral cancer
1 first- or second-degree relative with breast cancer at ≤50 years old, or bilateral breast cancer, and 1 first- or second-degree relative with ovarian cancer
3 cases of breast and ovarian cancer (at least 1 case of ovarian cancer) in first- or second-degree relatives
2 cases of ovarian cancer in first- or second-degree relatives
1 case of male breast cancer in a first- or second-degree relative if another first- or second-degree relative has (male or female) breast or ovarian cancer
Jewish families
Any of the following:
1 or more cases of breast cancer ≤50 years old in a first- or second-degree relative
1 or more cases of ovarian cancer at any age in a first- or second-degree relative
1 or more first- or second-degree relatives with breast cancer at any age, if another first- or second-degree relative has breast and/or ovarian cancer at any age
1 or more cases of male breast cancer in a first- or second-degree relative
Adapted from: Hampel H, Sweet K, Westman JA, et al. Referral for cancer genetics consultation: a review and compilation of risk assessment criteria. J Med Genet 2004; 41:81.
