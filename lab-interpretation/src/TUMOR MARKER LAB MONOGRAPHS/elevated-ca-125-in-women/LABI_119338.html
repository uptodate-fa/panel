Initial evaluation of elevated CA 125 in women*
This algorithm is intended for use with additional UpToDate content on ovarian cancer.
CA 125: cancer antigen 125; EOC: epithelial ovarian carcinoma; BRCA: breast cancer susceptibility gene.
​* The normal reference range for CA 125 is ≤35 units/mL. When serial CA 125 testing is performed in an individual patient, the same laboratory and assay should be used. Interpretation of a specific abnormal test result should be based upon the reference range reported with that result.
​¶ Screening for ovarian cancer should be limited to women with hereditary ovarian cancer syndrome. High-risk familial cancer syndromes include BRCA and Lynch syndrome.
