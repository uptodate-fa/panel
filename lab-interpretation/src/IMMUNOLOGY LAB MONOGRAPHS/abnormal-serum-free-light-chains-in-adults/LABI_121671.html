Specialty consultation for abnormal serum free light chains in adults
Clinical "red flags" that necessitate specialty consultation:
  • Bone pain
  • Unexplained fatigue/generalized weakness
  • Constitutional "B" symptoms (unintentional weight loss, fever, night sweats)
  • Neurologic symptoms (neuropathy, headache, dizziness, loss of vision/hearing)
  • Unexplained bleeding
  • Symptoms suggestive of amyloidosis (macroglossia, nephrotic range proteinuria, restrictive cardiomyopathy, unexplained elevated NT-proBNP)
  • Lymphadenopathy, hepatomegaly, or splenomegaly
  • Abnormal laboratory findings: anemia, elevated creatinine, hypercalcemia, serum or urine M-protein
This table is intended for use with additional UpToDate content on abnormal serum free light chains.
NT-proBNP: N-terminal pro-brain natriuretic peptide; M-protein: monoclonal protein.
